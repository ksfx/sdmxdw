# Einzelkrediterhebung (EKE)

Granular **loan-by-loan survey** of the Swiss National Bank (SNB). Modelled as a microdata container from the official data model (version 5).

| Aspect | Content |
|---|---|
| Reporting agents | Selected banks domiciled in Switzerland |
| Granularity | Instrument, counterparty, collateral and protection |
| Reporting reference date | Quarterly |

Use **Show relations** for the entity-relationship view; click an entity to display its attributes.

Survey documents: [SNB eMI](https://emi.snb.ch/)
