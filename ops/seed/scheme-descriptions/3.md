# AnaCredit

**Analytical Credit Datasets** of the European System of Central Banks (Regulation ECB/2016/13): loan-level data on credit to legal entities, harmonised across the euro area.

- Instrument, financial, counterparty-instrument, protection and accounting datasets
- Counterparty reference data (Register of Institutions and Affiliates Data, RIAD)
- In SDMxDW: demo data container modelled after the AnaCredit Reporting Manual

Source: [ECB – AnaCredit](https://www.ecb.europa.eu/stats/money_credit_banking/anacredit/html/index.en.html)
