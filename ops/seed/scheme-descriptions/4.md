# SNB surveys (eMI)

Aggregated **statistical surveys** of the Swiss National Bank collected via the eMI reporting platform, modelled as SDMX time series.

- **KRED – Kreditvolumenstatistik**: credit volume by borrower group, sector, size class and maturity. Each row of the official form is a combination of codes in the DSD key (see the dataflow's structure).
- Reporting forms and codelists: [SNB eMI – survey documents](https://emi.snb.ch/)

Revisions are kept as vintages: use **Show values as of** on a series page to compare with an earlier data state.
